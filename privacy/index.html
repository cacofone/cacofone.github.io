2. In short

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking services. It loads no fonts, scripts or content from other servers. Nothing is stored on your device or read from it, so no cookie banner is needed.

3. Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is served by GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you open the site, GitHub processes data in its server logs as a technical necessity, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, and browser and device information. According to GitHub, visitors' IP addresses are logged for security reasons. We do not receive these logs.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is delivering this website securely and reliably.

Data may be transferred to the USA. The transfer relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework of 10 July 2023, to the extent GitHub is certified under it, and otherwise on standard contractual clauses.

4. Domain and DNS at Cloudflare

The domain cacofone.com is managed through Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Cloudflare answers name lookups (DNS), and may process the IP addresses of the DNS servers that ask. Traffic to the website itself does not pass through Cloudflare. For email forwarding see section 5. Data may also be transferred to the USA here, and what section 3 says applies in the same way.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a working domain).

5. Contacting us by email

If you write to admin@cacofone.com, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer you. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for requests about a contract. We delete the data once your request is dealt with and no retention duty applies.

The address admin@cacofone.com is a forwarding address. Incoming email passes through Cloudflare Email Routing (Cloudflare, Inc., see section 4) and is delivered from there to the mailbox of the person responsible. Cloudflare processes the message as a technical necessity, including sender, recipient, subject and content, in order to deliver it. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a working email contact).
