Privacy Policy

Cacofone Streamer · Last updated 22 November 2026

Cacofone Streamer is a music player for your home network. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing about you and sends nothing to us.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information.

How the app uses your network

  • The app looks for media servers and players on your local network (using the DLNA and UPnP standards) so it can show and play your music.
  • Music and track information travel directly between your device and the devices on your network. They do not pass through any Cacofone server.
  • The app needs the local network and Wi-Fi permissions on your phone or tablet to do this.

Information stored on your device

The app may save settings, such as your preferred speaker or recently played items, on your device only. You can remove them at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through github.com/cacofone.
