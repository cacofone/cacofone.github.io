Cacofone Streamer

Your music library, playing on any speaker in the house. A DLNA & UPnP music player.

In development

What it will do

  • Finds your devices

    Discovers media servers and players on your home network automatically.

  • Browses your library

    Look through the albums, artists and playlists on your media server.

  • Plays anywhere

    Send music to network speakers, AV receivers and other DLNA players.

  • Private by design

    Works on your local network. No account, no ads, no tracking.
